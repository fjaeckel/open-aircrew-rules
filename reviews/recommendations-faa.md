# Recommendations: FAA decision rows

Eleven `faa.*` rows of the decision table in [README.md](README.md): one recommendation keeps the file's current choice (flight instructor regime), ten change it; none is low confidence, two are medium.
Evidence is official only (eCFR, Federal Register, FAA Office of the Chief Counsel interpretations, FAA advisory circulars and handbooks); these are recommendations for the owner, not approvals, and no `approved_by` field is touched.

Option numbers refer to the "Options" column of the decision table. Principles: P1 missing record data, P2 checks, P3 windows, P4 simulators and devices, P5 presentation-only rows; "text" means the regulation or an official interpretation settles the question directly.

## Findings beyond the listed options

- `faa.rating.instrument-airplane` / `ipc-restores`: the IPC that restores currency must be in an aircraft appropriate to the category or a device representative of it (61.57(d)(2)(i), (ii)); the file does not check the category of the IPC either. It should follow whatever is chosen for `any-powered-category`.
- `faa.shared.flight-review` / `who-and-endorsement-not-checked`: the same authority question applies to the `flight_review` event itself. 61.56(c)(1) requires an "authorized instructor", which 61.1 defines as the holder of a part 61 instructor certificate, so a review logged with a non-FAA instructor does not count. The event-authority field that option (2) needs would close this too.
- `faa.shared.passengers-day` / `class-or-type`: FAA single-engine land and sea remain separate classes; pooling must not merge them.
- The Williams letter carries no legible date; the year given is the one in the FAA file title.
