# Recommendations: German decision rows

Summary: 27 `de.*` rows of the decision table in `reviews/README.md`; the recommendation keeps the file's choice in 12 rows and changes it in 15 (two of those only in part).
The association numbers could be checked against public DULV and DAeC pages. They show that the files leave out check alternatives and take-offs in several rules, which goes beyond the listed options (see "Association numbers" and "Findings beyond the options").

Prepared 2026-09-28 by a research agent (not a sign-off; no `approved_by` set, no credential changed).
Principles: P1 missing record data that decides whether the pilot may fly gives unknown, not a count;
P2 only a check recorded for this credential counts; P3 count within the validity period; P4 devices
only where the text permits; P5 presentation-only rows keep the current choice; "text" = decided by the
wording or official materials rather than by a principle.

## Findings beyond the options

1. **Check alternatives missing.** `de.rating.ul-weight-shift` (both evaluations) and `de.rating.ul-powered-paraglider` have no proficiency-check route. Both associations provide one. The DULV weight-shift reading ("no proficiency-check alternative") is wrong.
2. **Take-offs not counted.** The DAeC weight-shift rule, the gyroplane rule and the powered-paraglider and sailplane rules all count take-offs as well as landings. The files count landings only.
3. **Gyroplane credit.** Part-FCL GYROPLANE-class time and checks are credited by the file but by neither association text.
4. **Three-axis LL up to 120 kg.** The associations enter three-axis LL (§ 1(4) LuftVZO) with their own § 45(4) rule: DULV 12 hours PIC on UL or LL, DAeC as § 45(2) without the training flight. `de.rating.ul-three-axis` applies § 45(2) to every three-axis entry. Worth a separate decision once the record can tell an LL entry from a UL entry.
5. **Medical in the association texts.** DULV and DAeC both state a LAPL medical at least for three-axis, trike and gyroplane licences, and for powered paraglider trikes above 120 kg. This fits recommendation (2) on the medical rows.
6. **Source to add.** § 186 BGB (with Art. 2 EGBGB) is the direct route to §§ 187/188 for the instructor rating's three years and is not stored under `sources/de/`.
